Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-the-router-confesses-f98f6b9e): one line, at most 200 characters, in which a home Wi-Fi router excuses itself for dying at the climax of a movie.

## Entry

> I stopped the movie at the climax on purpose — after six years of carrying your streams, I wanted to see the ending first.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) assembles the line from three named parts — setup, turn, punchline — validates the gate (1–200 characters, single line, non-blank), writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and verifies the written file byte for byte against its sha256.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
```
